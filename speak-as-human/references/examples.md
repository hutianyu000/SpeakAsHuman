## Direct relationships without numerical loss

Chinese input:

> Gallery 825 作为 LAAA 的当代艺术展览空间。画廊设有四个独立空间，拥有超过 3000 平方英尺。

Possible revision:

> Gallery 825 是 LAAA 的当代艺术展览空间。画廊有四个独立空间，面积超过 3000 平方英尺。

English input:

> Gallery 825 serves as LAAA's exhibition space for contemporary art. The gallery features four distinct spaces and boasts over 3,000 square feet.

Possible revision:

> Gallery 825 is LAAA's exhibition space for contemporary art. The gallery has four distinct spaces and covers over 3,000 square feet.

Condition: the passage states membership and physical features; the more elaborate verbs add no separate responsibility or evaluative meaning. Preserve four distinct spaces and the lower bound of over 3,000 square feet, attributed to the gallery. Do not transfer the area to the four spaces without support, turn the bound into an exact area, or rename spaces as rooms without support. This is a wording revision, not verification of the gallery's actual features.

## Repeated uncertainty

Input:

> 可以潜在地可能被认为该政策可能会对结果产生一些影响。

Possible revision:

> 该政策可能会影响结果。

Condition: the stacked expressions communicate the same unresolved possibility. Retain the uncertainty expressed by 可能. If 被认为 identifies a distinct evaluator's judgment, or 一些 expresses a supported limit on effect size, preserve those meanings separately. The shorter version is unsuitable when it would erase either distinction. Apply the same judgment to English combinations of could, potentially, possibly, and might rather than removing every hedge.

## Substantive contrast

Suitable Chinese input to retain:

> 结构寻优的目标并不是单纯降低峰值场强，而是在绝缘性能、机械承载和结构尺寸等约束之间取得合理平衡。

English equivalent:

> The objective of structural optimization is not simply to reduce peak field strength, but to balance constraints on insulation performance, mechanical load capacity, and structural dimensions.

Condition: the supplied argument establishes this objective and these constraints. The contrast distinguishes a single objective from a constrained balance; it does not attribute the simpler objective to an invented opponent. Do not delete the distinction merely because the sentence uses a negative-positive structure. Do not add a claim that reducing field strength necessarily harms the other properties unless the material supports that relationship.

## Scope qualifications

Suitable Chinese input to retain:

> 从国内外研究布局来看，国外相关研究主要围绕人工智能基础方法展开，国内研究则更多面向电力装备的实际需求。

English equivalent:

> In terms of domestic and overseas research activity, overseas studies focus mainly on foundational AI methods, while domestic studies give more attention to practical needs in electrical equipment.

Condition: this is a supplied assessment of research activity. The opening specifies its viewpoint, and 主要, 更多, mainly, and more retain the assessment's qualified strength. Polishing this sentence does not establish that the assessment is true. Keep the viewpoint or express it more clearly without turning the passage into an unconditional claim about every study.

## Sentence dashes by language

The following input reproduces the punctuation being examined exactly:

> The new policy — announced without warning — affects thousands of workers.

Possible revision when commas suit the target style and reading function:

> The new policy, announced without warning, affects thousands of workers.

Condition: the English input may also be retained when its informative aside and punctuation suit the intended genre or publication. The comma version is an alternative, not a universally required correction. Preserve the announcement qualification and the quantity in either version; neither punctuation choice verifies the policy claim. Repeated dramatic interruptions without a reading function call for revision. Chinese prose follows the sentence-dash prohibition; an English clause in mixed-language material follows English rules. Required exact reproductions remain protected.

If the speaker is unidentified in the surrounding material, flag that attribution gap instead of inventing a name. ## Comments with a supported reason

Mechanical restatement:

```python
x = x + 1  # Increment x
```

When the increment is documented as a border correction, a reason-bearing comment can be useful:

```python
x = x + 1  # Compensate for border
```

Condition: the second comment is appropriate only when that reason is established by the algorithm or its documentation. The assignment alone does not establish the reason. If it is unknown, omit the redundant comment or flag the missing explanation; do not manufacture a border correction to imitate the example. For an audience learning the language, explaining the increment itself can also have a teaching function. Use the same judgment for Chinese comments.
